token-risk

Enter a Solana or EVM token address, or a ticker, to get a risk score from 0 to 100. The score is based on contract permissions, liquidity, holder concentration, trading activity and age, and each deduction is listed with its reason.

Scoring

CategoryWeightWhat is checked
Contract30%Mint and freeze authority (Solana), honeypot, taxes, owner permissions (EVM)
Liquidity25%Pooled USD, liquidity vs market cap, LP locked or burned
Holders20%Top 10 and largest wallet share, wallets linked to the deployer
Trading15%Buy/sell ratio, volume vs liquidity, recent price moves
Age10%Pool age, listed website and socials, paid promotion

Some findings cap the total: live freeze authority at 35, live mint authority at 50, honeypot at 5, liquidity under $10K at 40.

Data from DexScreener, RugCheck, GoPlus and GeckoTerminal. Not financial advice. Source: github.com/hpark236/token-risk
